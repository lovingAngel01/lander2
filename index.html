Why now

The decade of rebuilding is already underway.

Energy, food, housing and mobility are being redesigned in real time — in cities that cannot wait for perfect plans. CANOPY is where the people doing that work meet the people funding it.

What you get

Four days of signal, zero filler.

Every CANOPY session is built around a deliverable, not a slide deck. You arrive with a question and leave with something working.

01

Keynotes that argue a position

No panels of five. Two voices, one contested claim, and a moderated Q&A that runs long on purpose.

02

Breakouts by domain, not by job title

Six tracks run in parallel — grid, buildings, food, water, land and capital — so you meet peers working the same problem.

03

Labs with a working artefact

Model a district heat network, price a retrofit, draft a procurement rule. Depart with a repo or a signed pilot.

04

Rooms you can only get in person

Investor office hours, municipal roundtables and a speaker lounge that unlocks with any tier above Field.
